Command Centre privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026.

What it accesses

Read-only access to the signed-in YouTube account (youtube.readonly): the list of subscribed channels, saved playlists, and public video details.

How it is used

Only to show new videos and playlist progress on a dashboard that runs on the owner's own PC.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens and fetched results are stored only on that PC. They are not shared with anyone, sold, or sent to any other service. The app never posts, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.

Command Centre's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
